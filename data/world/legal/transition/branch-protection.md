# Schedule 1 — The Rules of Required Approval

*(the branch-protection instrument; Constitutional Approval Act, Schedule 1)*

## 1. The mainline

The mainline of the federal archive may be advanced only by the Keeper of
the Federal Rolls (office: `federal-archivist`). No other office, and no
person without office, may advance it.

## 2. The municipal mainlines

The mainline of a city's archive (`municipal/<city>/**`) may be advanced
only by that city's archivist (office: `local-archivist-<city>`).

## 3. Required review

No proposal may be ratified without constitutional review by the
Constitutional Council. A proposal the Council finds incompatible with
the constitution shall not be ratified.

## 4. Formal checks

No proposal may be ratified while the Mechanical Magistrate reports a
formal defect (Mechanical Magistracy Act, Article 2).

## 5. Realization

The platform realizes these rules mechanically by protecting the branches
herein named. The platform's protection records the law; it does not make
it.
