# Assigned Legal Authority Act

## Preamble

Whereas the archive host is a mere mechanical repository of the federal
rolls, knowing refs and nothing of offices, petitions or approvals; and
whereas authority over the corpus resides in the offices of the Concord
alone, and no office may be presumed: be it enacted that the schedule of
assigned legal authority shall be recorded in the corpus itself, so that
what the offices hold by the constitution may be checked mechanically.

## Article 1 — Purpose

This Act assigns legal authority over the paths of the federal archive to
the offices of the Concord, so that no change may enter the corpus without
the authority of the office to which the path is assigned.

## Article 2 — Provisions

1. The Schedule of Assigned Legal Authority, annexed to this Act as
   Schedule 1 (the CODEOWNERS instrument), is established and has the
   force of law.
2. Authority follows the schedule: an office holds authority over the
   paths assigned to it, and no other.
3. A change to the schedule is an amendment of this Act, enacted through
   the common procedure.

## Article 3 — Enforcement

The platform may realize the schedule mechanically, requiring the review
of a proposed change by the incumbent of each assigned office. Such
mechanical realization records, and does not create, legal authority:
the authority remains with the offices, and the platform merely refuses
what the schedule does not admit.

## Article 4 — Relation to Existing Law

This Act gives effect to the offices established at the founding — the
Keeper of the Federal Rolls, the domain experts, the jurists of the
Constitutional Council, and the city archivists — and is consistent with
the founding constitution (`constitution/01-foundation.md`).

## Article 5 — Entry into Force

This Act enters into force upon its ratification by the Keeper of the
Federal Rolls.
