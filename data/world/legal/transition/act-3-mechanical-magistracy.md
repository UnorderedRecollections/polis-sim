# Mechanical Magistracy Act

## Preamble

Whereas the forms of the Concord are many, and their examination is
burdensome upon the offices; and whereas a mechanical office was foreseen
at the founding, dormant until codified: be it enacted that the
Mechanical Magistrate is erected — software, not a person — to check the
form of every proposal and to report its findings, deciding nothing.

## Article 1 — Purpose

This Act activates the office of the Mechanical Magistrate (`office:
mechanical-magistrate`) and defines the formal checks it executes.

## Article 2 — Provisions

1. The office of the Mechanical Magistrate is activated, holding the
   powers `execute:formal-checks` and `report:commit-status`.
2. The Magistrate executes the formal checks annexed to this Act as
   Schedule 1 (the `.woodpecker.yml` instrument): that every act
   identifies itself, its jurisdiction, its proposer and its entry into
   force; that its references resolve to law existing in the corpus; and
   that it does not conflict with constitutional provisions.
3. The Magistrate decides nothing. Its findings are reported to the
   offices, and the offices decide.
4. A proposal may not be ratified while the Magistrate reports a formal
   defect.

## Article 3 — Enforcement

The Magistrate's checks run automatically upon every proposed change to
the archive, and its findings are recorded with the proposal.

## Article 4 — Relation to Existing Law

This Act activates the office foreseen at the founding, and is consistent
with the schedule of authority (Assigned Legal Authority Act) and the
rules of required approval (Constitutional Approval Act).

## Article 5 — Entry into Force

This Act enters into force upon its ratification by the Keeper of the
Federal Rolls.
